System · adapted from the Legacy engine

From a WhatsApp message to a lead

WhatsApp One shared number for both brands. Provider (ChakraHQ or Meta) still to decide.
Message router Merges quick texts (8 s), checks staff takeover, routes couriers, suppliers and job seekers to Bukan Lead.
AI turn (Claude) Prompt + full catalog with tiers. Tools: hitung_harga, resolve_link, web_search. Answers through respond_to_customer.
Reply checks Strict price check, tone, catalog claims, fit judge. Fails → redo, then handoff.
Send Text, up to 3 reference photos, price list file, PO form link. Code attaches every link.
After each turn The customer's details merge into their profile: name, business, brands (union), items keyed by product + size, so moving from paper to boxes keeps both.
Handoff to staff Custom jobs, design files, "mau bicara dengan orang". The bot asks the next missing detail while staff get a push alert with all six details.
Lead pass, every 10 minutes Reads quiet chats: lead or not, stage (Baru → Dihubungi → Penawaran → Menang / Kalah), Hot/Cold, brands, items. Won/Lost need a quoted customer message.
